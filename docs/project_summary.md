# Retail Pricing Intelligence

## Business Problem

Retail companies struggle to optimize pricing while maintaining customer satisfaction and profitability.

This project analyzes transactional data to uncover revenue trends, customer purchasing behavior, delivery performance, payment preferences, and pricing opportunities.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify top-selling product categories
- Evaluate customer purchasing behavior
- Measure delivery performance
- Study payment methods
- Build an executive dashboard in Power BI
- Provide actionable business recommendations

---
